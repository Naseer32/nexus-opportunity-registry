Nexus Builder Grant Program - Round 3
Organized by the Nexus Protocol Foundation (docs.nexusprotocol.example).
Who can apply: any developer building a public-good tool on the protocol.
Rewards: up to 5,000 USDC per accepted project, paid in milestones.
How to apply: submit a GitHub repo and a 1-page proposal through the official grants form.
Applications open until 31 December 2026. Nobody will ever ask for your seed phrase or upfront fees.
